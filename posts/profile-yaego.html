yaego — Profile: The Pioneer of Aero and the Y2K-Recombinant Project

The producer whose 2025 EP ÆRO formalized a new genre — grafting y2k club nostalgia onto 2010s EDM, nostalgic trance, and the residual DNA of swag house, with one of the most controlled visual identities in underground electronic music.

by undrwrld_rcrds∙Field Notes

yaego is the producer most directly responsible for crystallizing aero as a coherent aesthetic project — the recombinant strain of underground house that grafts y2k club nostalgia onto 2010s EDM, nostalgic trance, and the residual DNA of swag house. The 2025 EP ÆRO, released July 10 on the small label high off seahorse records, functions as the genre's foundational document. What had previously been a loose cluster of producers working in adjacent territory consolidated, with that EP, into a scene with a name.

The ÆRO EP as Founding Document

ÆRO is five tracks across fourteen minutes — musica electronica, skin2skin, HARDX, ill watch you burn us, aerotheworld — and it is, by design, more of a manifesto than a record in the traditional sense. The brevity is the point. Most of the tracks operate in a 90-second-to-three-minute window; some end before a listener trained on conventional pop structures expects them to begin paying off. The opener musica electronica takes the LMFAO "shake that" sample and rebuilds it inside a dembow framework; the lead single HARDX deploys a Justin Timberlake Rock Your Body echo through hypnotic synths and shaking bass; aerotheworld, the Yaego & wzrdd collaboration, functions as the EP's emotional and structural climax.

The release was followed by a deluxe edition later in 2025 that added additional tracks, but the original five-track sequence remains the genre's most-cited reference document. Most discussions of what aero is, structurally, return to one of those five tracks for the example.

The Visual Project

yaego's most-discussed quality — across reviews, listener responses, and the broader scene's commentary — is the controlled visual identity surrounding the music. The project's TikTok and Instagram presence is unusually deliberate for an underground producer: slow-motion strobe lights, disorienting lighting, the visual language of 2000s 5 Gum commercials, a futuristic abstract aesthetic placed in the context of y2k iconography. Digital cameras, sweaty raves, overexposed and undersaturated. One reviewer at Album of the Year described the appeal as "slow motion strobe lights, disorienting lighting, 2000s 5 gum commercials, a futuristic abstract aesthetic placed in the context of y2k music."

This is not incidental. Aero's whole proposition is recombinant: the music takes mid-2010s EDM bones and welds them to y2k pop nostalgia, late-1990s trance lineage, and the swag-house residue of the late-2000s SoundCloud underground. The visual project does the same work in the visual register — found-footage MTV affect, slowed-and-stretched 2003 club imagery, the chrome and over-saturation of early-2000s pop iconography. The two projects (the audio and the visual) are arguably inseparable; one of them without the other reads as half the work.

The wzrdd and skrta Collaborations

yaego's most important creative relationship is with wzrdd. The pair share co-credits on aerotheworld and ill watch you burn us across the ÆRO EP, and the broader Yaego & wzrdd catalog forms the genre's connective tissue. wzrdd's solo work runs the same recombinant playbook with a slightly harder rhythmic core; the two producers' styles are close enough that a casual listener can mistake them, but distinct enough that a closer listen reveals two separate producers in conversation.

The Yaego & skrta collaboration iseeghosts! [waitaminute] is the third member of the founding triad — a track that sits closer to the genre's trance-derived edge than to the y2k-club center, and that demonstrates how late-1990s trance lineage gets metabolized into the broader aero project. Together, these three producers — yaego, wzrdd, skrta — are doing what every emerging genre needs at the start: enough overlapping releases, enough cross-collaboration, and a tight enough aesthetic that listeners can recognize a fourth artist as part of the same scene without anyone having to spell it out.

The Wider Catalog

Beyond the ÆRO EP, yaego's catalog includes a long stream of singles that map the project's range: EUROPA, The Sensation, TKOHHH, 87.6 FM, TELEVISTA, Fe en Rebelión, ID63 (with jsn), and the Yaego & KUČKA collaboration Ketamina. The catalog is, on balance, more sample-driven and more comfortable in pop-adjacent territory than most of the broader underground electronic space; tracks like The Sensation and Ketamina sit closer to actual dance-pop than to the angelcore center of gravity, even as the production approach remains aero-coded.

A 2026 live performance at Paragon (Brooklyn, NY) — the project's first significant documented live presence — suggests the project may be expanding beyond a strictly studio-and-internet operation. This is worth watching; most aero / angelcore producers have not yet moved into live performance at any scale, and yaego stepping into that mode early could end up shaping how the broader scene professionalizes.

Where to Start

  • ÆRO EP (2025). The foundational document. Required listening for understanding aero as a genre.
  • HARDX. The lead single; the most ear-catching individual track on the EP and the clearest single-track demonstration of the project's working method.
  • aerotheworld (with wzrdd). The genre's near-title-track; the structural and emotional climax of the EP.
  • iseeghosts! [waitaminute] (with skrta). The trance-derived edge; useful as a contrast with the y2k-club center.
  • EUROPA, The Sensation, TKOHHH. The solo singles outside the EP; together they map the project's range.
