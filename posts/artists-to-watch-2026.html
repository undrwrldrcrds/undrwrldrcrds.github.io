Twenty Artists Defining the Angelcore / Aero Continuum in 2026

From the LONOWN / akiaura working partnership to the yaego / wzrdd aero project, plus the satellite producers populating ARCADIA's playlists — a working list of the names worth knowing as the scene crystallizes.

by undrwrld_rcrds∙Artists

The angelcore / aero / outsider house cluster is small enough that a list of its most important producers is actually feasible. What follows is not a strict ranking — the genre is too internally varied for that to mean much — but a working roll call of the artists whose work has shaped the sound's current iteration, drawn from compilation lineups, editorial playlist regulars, and the producers other producers in the scene consistently cite.

akiaura

saint petersburg · angelcore / witch house

The single most consequential producer in the contemporary angelcore lineage. Coming up through the corecore and witch-house-adjacent SoundCloud underground, akiaura broke through with the 2023 EP Ketamine Girl and the viral single Sleepwalker — which across its various slowed and remixed versions has accumulated something on the order of 288 million Spotify streams and 600M+ TikTok video uses. Start with Ketamine Girl, then the LONOWN-collaborative Destruction Age (2024) for the album-length statement.

Sleepwalker (182M streams)

LONOWN

russia · angelcore

akiaura's most frequent collaborator and the co-architect of the angelcore sound. The pair's 2024 album Destruction Age is the genre's foundational text; LONOWN's solo tracks (starly, plus the broader catalog across the scene's release ecosystem) are at the more rhythmically intact end of the spectrum and tend to sit at the front of any contemporary angelcore mixtape. Functions as the genre's compositional anchor.

Spotify · 6.7M monthly listeners
AVANGARD (107M streams)
LONOWN · 2024
listen

yaego

prince george's county, md · aero / electroclash

The pioneer of aero and the producer responsible for crystallizing the y2k-recombinant sound as a coherent aesthetic project. The 2025 EP ÆRO is the genre's foundational document; the broader catalog (EUROPA, The Sensation, TKOHHH, the Ketamina collaboration with KUČKA) maps the project's range. Visual presence on TikTok is unusually controlled for the scene — slow-motion strobe lights, overexposed-and-undersaturated photography, an abstract futurism filtered through y2k iconography — and the music sounds like the natural soundtrack to that visual world.

Spotify · 491K monthly listeners
Fe en Rebelión (2.7M)
yaego · 2025

wzrdd

undisclosed · aero

yaego's most frequent collaborator and arguably aero's co-author. aerotheworld, ill watch you burn us, and the broader Yaego & wzrdd discography form the genre's connective tissue. wzrdd's solo work runs the same recombinant playbook with a slightly harder rhythmic core. Worth tracking independently of the yaego collaborations.

Spotify · aero collaborator
aerotheworld (w/ yaego)

skrta

toronto · aero / trance-leaning

The third member of the aero founding triad, sitting closer to the genre's trance-derived edge. The Yaego & skrta collaboration iseeghosts! [waitaminute] is one of the more rhythmically intact entries in the aero canon and a useful demonstration of how trance lineage gets metabolized into the broader project.

DJ Anemia

russia · angelcore / curator

Operates simultaneously as producer and curator. The ANGELCORE PLAYLIST mixtape series — particularly the DJ Anemia & crier collaborative volumes — has functioned as the scene's most consistent editorial document outside of the broader canonical apparatus release calendar. Tracks tend toward the more dissolved end of the genre spectrum, leaning closer to ambient than to dance music proper.

crier

undisclosed · angelcore

Frequent DJ Anemia collaborator and an increasingly visible producer in their own right. The DJ Anemia / Crier / sixnite track with you is a useful entry point. Production is more melodically forward than most of the scene — the vocal hooks land harder, the patience is less pronounced.

angelrot

bay area, ca · multi-genre electronic

One of the more visible Bay Area producers operating in the broader angelcore / opiumcore space, with roughly 100,000 monthly Spotify listeners and a presence on the "Angelcore: Compiled" compilation. The catalog is unusually broad for a scene this niche — Soft Collapse (2025) functions as the most fully realized album-length statement, while singles like iconic, STAYWITHME, and eurosummer (all 2026) demonstrate the producer's willingness to move between the genre's softer and more uptempo poles. Self-described as multi-genre, which reads accurately: tracks like Never Me sit closer to dreampop, while tonic moves into harder territory. A useful contemporary single-artist entry point for a listener trying to get oriented in the current scene.

Spotify · 104K monthly listeners
iconic
angelrot · 2026

Baby Jane

los angeles · gothic house / angelcore-adjacent

Sofia Bella Galuz — born 2001, based in Los Angeles, singer-songwriter and producer working in a self-described "gothic house" register that fuses witch house, hardstyle, and ethereal pop. Featured on the canonical Deathwish track alongside akiaura and LONOWN. Operates at the more vocally-forward end of angelcore — the lyric is more present, the song more legible as a song, even as the surrounding production stays well inside genre conventions. A useful entry point for listeners coming to angelcore from a more traditional pop or alt-pop background.

Spotify · gothic house / LA
A Grave Marked Strange

VNXIOUS

undisclosed · angelcore

One of the scene's most prolific producers and one of its most reliable curators — the prodvnxious-tagged YouTube angelcore mixes are a parallel canon to the established editorial center playlists. Solo tracks lean toward the more compressed and immediate end of the genre; the release cadence alone has made VNXIOUS a fixture on every relevant playlist.

Hugeloud

undisclosed · angelcore / electroclash

One of the bigger names you haven't heard of yet — 314K monthly listeners on Spotify, top track Destiny sitting at 2.8 million streams, and the kind of catalog depth that signals a producer who's been working this register since before it had a name. The 2026 release calendar — Empty streets, Subtle Attack, Dancefloor, Jumpstyle 2006 — pulls in jumpstyle, dirty electro house, and the kind of cosmic-club register that bridges angelcore's more melodic side to the broader contemporary dance ecology. BURN FOR YOU and its slowed edition together cleared 4 million streams. The project's been on the official Angelcore Spotify playlist since the playlist had room for anyone.

Kaikobad

undisclosed · outsider house / lo-fi

427K monthly listeners and a top track — Turn Echo — sitting at 7.1 million streams. The catalog reads as outsider house executed at the more melodic, listener-friendly end of the lineage; the Cult Member Archive, wiv, and Gygas adjacencies on the Spotify recommendation graph put Kaikobad squarely in the contemporary outsider house cohort even when individual tracks lean closer to ambient or downtempo. The Turn Echo (Slowed Version) at 2.1M streams demonstrates the parallel-version distribution model working at scale in the outsider house corner too — not just angelcore.

XEOE

undisclosed · aero / hardwave

Pronounced shay-oh. 1.1 million monthly listeners and a catalog whose top track — TRIPPLEX - Ultra Slowed — has crossed 15.5 million streams. The project sits at the louder, harder edge of the aero adjacency, with BRUTAL at 8.4M and EGO - SUPER SLOWED at 5M. Recent release Angel Radio 2 (early 2026) makes the angelcore-adjacent positioning explicit; the rest of the catalog is more hardwave-coded but operates in the same emotional register. By raw streaming numbers, one of the largest individual artists in the entire scene-adjacent space.

CNQR+

undisclosed · angelcore / house

Stylized with the trailing plus. 101K monthly listeners, top track In your soul at 938K streams, and a catalog that pulls about evenly from angelcore and the more rhythmically intact house tradition. The 2026 release cadence is fast — Core4, Aireco, Melithe, Cordurance, Glass life, So close all dropped within months of each other — and the project keeps a presence on the Hardwave Task Force compilation track listing alongside the dedicated angelcore curations. Listed contact is cnqrmusic@gmail.com if you're trying to reach the project directly.

The Dark Architect

undisclosed · dark minimal house

Smaller numbers, denser project — 10K monthly listeners, but five full albums (THE SORROWS OF OUR NATURE, RATHER BE FEARED, THAN LOVED, DAYS AFTER DEATH, PARKING GARAGE ANGELS, The Dark Side of Intimacy) and a release rhythm that doesn't look like most working producers'. Top track PISCEAN BLOOD at 131K. The aesthetic is dark minimal house — slow, sparse, mostly mid-tempo, with album titles and track titles (SAGITTARIUS LILITH, IS THIS THE DEMON YOU SENT?, SANRIO LUVS CIGARETTES) that do a lot of the emotional framing before the first kick lands. Producer's own framing per the bio: "my music contains pieces of my soul, the deepest places of my emotional state." Take that or leave it — the project sits at the genuinely-underground end of the spectrum, and the catalog rewards the patience.

Gygas

undisclosed · angelcore / outsider house — ARCADIA / IMMINENT

Per the Spotify bio: "label: ARCADIA / IMMINENT." That puts Gygas in genuinely canonical-tier company — one of the few producers on this list who's officially tagged to the foundational label apparatus. 192K monthly listeners, top track Loveless at 1.16M streams, a catalog stretching back to the 2018 Tapes from the Attic album. The project operates across multiple registers — straight angelcore, outsider house, the GHG NIGHTDRIVE / Rally House playlist circuit — but the through-line is a kind of patient vibe-construction that resists easy categorization. Going to be one of the more interesting trajectories to watch as ARCADIA / IMMINENT keeps building out its roster beyond the canonical names.

Spotify · 192.4K monthly listeners

wiv

undisclosed · outsider house / lo-fi house

One of the largest individual artists in the broader outsider house ecosystem — 1.37 million monthly listeners, top track i love u. at 24.8 million streams, the slowed edition another 12.9 million on top. The project has spawned its own micro-genre tag ("wivcore") and the broader catalog — fragments of a dream (2024), quiet disappearance (2026), borrowed will — operates in the more melancholic and ambient-leaning corner of lo-fi house. Fans-also-like includes Cult Member and Kaikobad, which is the clean confirmation that wiv sits in the same outsider house family even when the individual tracks lean closer to dreampop than to dance music proper.

How to Use This List

The honest answer: open Spotify, queue up Destruction Age, follow it with the ARCADIA-curated ANGELCORE playlists, and let the algorithm sort it out from there. The scene's social geography is dense enough that any one of these names will lead you to the others within an hour. The list above is less a starting point than a way of confirming you've found the right room.

One useful exercise: pick the artist on this list whose work you respond to most immediately, then look at the "Fans also like" section on their Spotify profile. Most of the genre's working producers will be one degree from any other. The scene operates as a single small graph.

Names worth tracking that didn't make the main list but consistently appear on the relevant playlists: DJ Pointless, deadwinter, riserayss, angeltears, ProdChxn, Crisys, fadevɇil, Oyabe, Krembuur, fervnt. Most are one degree from the names above on Spotify's recommendation graph.
