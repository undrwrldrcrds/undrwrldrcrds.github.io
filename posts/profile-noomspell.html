noomspell — Profile: The IMMINENT-Adjacent Producer at 16K Listeners

A short profile of one of the genre's lower-visibility but more compositionally controlled producers — currently sitting at 16K monthly Spotify listeners and the kind of release calendar that suggests a project still in its early consolidation phase.

by undrwrld_rcrds∙Field Notes

noomspell occupies the kind of footprint in the contemporary angelcore scene that most producers in the genre actually inhabit — the long tail of producers working at single-digit-thousands to low-five-figures of monthly listeners, releasing across the IMMINENT-aligned and broader angelcore-adjacent release infrastructure, and slowly accumulating the kind of catalog that, if the project sticks with it, eventually gets them into the satellite tier proper. At 16,150 monthly Spotify listeners as of mid-2026, noomspell is not yet a commercial force in the scene; the catalog, however, suggests that the project is doing the right structural work to get there.

The 2025 Release Calendar

The most useful single observation about noomspell is the release cadence. The 2025 calendar alone includes paranoida, alternate heaven, ereality, slasher, romancyde, Don't need you, angeldust, and stasis — eight separate releases across a single year, mostly single-track and short-EP formats. This is the working cadence the angelcore scene has converged on for emerging producers, and noomspell has internalized it with above-average discipline. The catalog has the kind of forward momentum that the algorithm tends to reward: enough releases to populate a "Featuring noomspell Radio" playlist with substantive material, enough variety across them to demonstrate compositional range, enough consistency in the working palette to read as a single coherent project rather than a producer-of-the-week.

The single-track distribution numbers are also informative. alternate heaven - Slowed sits at 122,130 plays — comfortably above the original (82,534) — which confirms the project is running the parallel-version distribution model that the broader scene runs on. ereality at 91,230 and paranoida at 39,204 fill out the catalog's top end. None of these are scene-defining numbers, but they're the kind of numbers that, in aggregate across a sustained release calendar, build the listener base a producer needs to graduate from the long tail into the broader roster.

The Compositional Method

What's audible across the catalog — and what distinguishes noomspell from most other producers at this listener count — is a willingness to let tracks operate at their natural length without padding. The working tracks are short. alternate heaven and paranoida both clock in under three and a half minutes; the longer cuts on the catalog don't exceed four. This is, in the contemporary scene, the structurally correct choice. Angelcore is a genre whose tracks tend to fail when they overstay their welcome, and producers who learn to trim early tend to develop faster than those who don't.

The bio reads "block out the world //" — which is, by some distance, the most useful three words you can put on an angelcore artist page. The catalog backs the framing up: most of the project's tracks operate in the genre's solitary, atmosphere-forward register, with vocal contributions processed past easy legibility and harmonic content that does its emotional work through repetition and patience rather than through dramatic structural moves.

Where the Project Sits

noomspell is, structurally, a project to watch rather than a project to listen to as a complete catalog. The 16K-listener footprint puts it well below the visibility threshold most listeners arrive at the genre through; the release cadence suggests a project that will, if it sustains the current trajectory, end up considerably more visible within a year. For listeners building out their angelcore listening graph beyond the canonical akiaura / LONOWN axis and the immediate scene's working middle (Crisys, ProdChxn, Krembuur, fervnt), noomspell is a useful next stop.

Where to Start

  • alternate heaven. Both original and slowed; the slowed version is the project's most-streamed single track.
  • paranoida. The most recent strong single. Demonstrates the project's compositional method at its most confident.
  • ereality. Mid-catalog atmospheric cut.
  • The 2025 catalog top-to-bottom. Reasonable to listen through in sequence; the catalog is compact enough that the time investment is small.
