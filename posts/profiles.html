Artist Profiles

Long-form coverage of the producers shaping the contemporary angelcore, aero, and outsider house scenes.

Canonical Tier

akiaura — Saint Petersburg / angelcore

The Saint Petersburg-based producer whose 2023 single Sleepwalker reframed the scope of what an underground angelcore release could become — and whose collaborative discography with LONOWN forms the genre's canonical core. 11 min read

LONOWN — Russia / angelcore

The producer whose collaborative work with akiaura forms the foundation of contemporary angelcore — and whose solo catalog has quietly crossed 6.7 million monthly Spotify listeners. 10 min read

Aero Pioneers

yaego — Prince George's County, MD / aero

The underground electronic producer whose 2025 EP ÆRO formalized aero as a coherent aesthetic project — grafting y2k club nostalgia onto 2010s EDM, nostalgic trance, and the residual DNA of swag house. 11 min read

wzrdd — undisclosed / aero

yaego's closest collaborator and arguably aero's co-author. aerotheworld, ill watch you burn us, and the broader Yaego & wzrdd discography form the genre's connective tissue. 8 min read

Curators and Producer-Curators

DJ Anemia — Russia / angelcore curator

The producer-curator whose ANGELCORE PLAYLIST mixtape series, alongside crier, has functioned as one of the scene's most consistent editorial documents — and whose own track archangel has become a canonical reference point. 8 min read

Satellite Tier

Crisys — Azurite Records' Lone Knight

The angelcore producer at 102K monthly Spotify listeners — frequent collaborator with ProdChxn and Krembuur, anchor of Azurite Records' working roster. 9 min read

angelrot — Bay Area / multi-genre electronic

The Bay Area electronic producer whose 104K monthly listeners and unusually wide catalog make the project one of the scene's clearest contemporary entry points. 10 min read

noomspell — IMMINENT-adjacent / angelcore

One of the genre's lower-visibility but more compositionally controlled producers — at 16K monthly listeners with a patient release calendar that suggests a project still consolidating. 7 min read

Outsider House

Cult Member — Victoria, BC / outsider house

Liam Hayden, whose 2019 cassette release Ethernet became one of the more genuine cult-hit albums of the lo-fi house era, and whose catalog continues to anchor the contemporary outsider house lineage. 10 min read
